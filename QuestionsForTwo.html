Two hearts, two perspectives, endless conversations.

A strong relationship is built not only through the moments we share but also through the conversations we have.

There is always something new to discover about each other, even after spending a long time together.

This space is designed to help us understand each other's thoughts, emotions, expectations, dreams, and perspectives.

Getting to Know Each Other Better

01. What makes you feel most loved?

02. What is your happiest childhood memory?

03. What is something that always makes you smile?

04. What is one thing you wish more people understood about you?

05. What makes you feel emotionally safe?

Understanding Our Relationship

06. What do you appreciate most about our relationship?

07. When do you feel closest to me?

08. What can I do to make you feel more appreciated?

09. What is one thing we should improve in our communication?

10. How can we handle disagreements more maturely?

Dreams and the Future

11. What kind of life would you love to build?

12. Which country would you love to visit with me?

13. What does your ideal home look like?

14. What are your biggest personal ambitions?

15. What traditions would you like us to create?

Faith and Personal Growth

16. What does having God at the center of a relationship mean to you?

17. What Bible passage has influenced your life the most?

18. How can we encourage each other spiritually?

19. What does a God-honoring relationship look like to you?

20. What kind of people do we want to become?

A Little Fun

21. Who is more likely to fall asleep during a movie?

22. Who takes longer to get ready?

23. If we could travel anywhere tomorrow, where would we go?

24. What would our perfect weekend look like?

25. What is one funny thing you will never forget about us?

Let's Keep Discovering Each Other

Let's never stop asking, listening, learning, and discovering new things about each other.
