Building a relationship with God at the center.

We believe that a meaningful relationship requires more than affection and emotions. It requires patience, understanding, forgiveness, wisdom, and a willingness to grow together.

Above everything else, we want our relationship to honor God.

This space is dedicated to our spiritual journey, the lessons we learn from Scripture, the prayers we share, and the encouragement we give one another as we continue growing in faith.

Our Favorite Bible Verses

Ecclesiastes 4:9–10

"Two are better than one..."

A reminder that companionship brings encouragement, strength, and support.

1 Corinthians 13:4–7

Love is patient and kind. It does not envy or boast.

A reminder that genuine love is demonstrated through character and actions.

Proverbs 3:5–6

Trust in the Lord with all your heart.

A reminder to seek God's direction rather than depending only on our own understanding.

Colossians 3:14

"And above all these put on love, which binds everything together in perfect harmony."

A reminder that love should guide how we treat one another.

Prayers for Each Other

  • Pray for each other's health and protection.
  • Pray for wisdom in making important decisions.
  • Pray for success in our studies and careers.
  • Pray for patience and understanding.
  • Pray for spiritual maturity.
  • Pray for God's guidance concerning our future.

Our Spiritual Goals

  1. Encourage each other to read the Bible.
  2. Pray for each other regularly.
  3. Learn to forgive and communicate with humility.
  4. Develop self-control and patience.
  5. Keep God at the center of our individual lives and relationship.

Our Prayer

Lord, teach us to love with patience, speak with kindness, forgive sincerely, and walk in Your wisdom. Help us become people who reflect Your character in the way we treat one another. May our relationship bring glory to You. Amen.

Two hearts growing in love, two lives growing in faith.
